# Revenue by film category

```sql category_revenue_summary
select
    name as category,
    sum(amount) as total_revenue
from sakila.category_revenue
group by name
order by total_revenue desc;
```

This chart creates a visulazation to show the total revenue generated by each film category. By linking rentals and payments back to film categories, we can understand wihch genres perform best financially. This insight can inform inventory decisions, marketing focus and  other strategies.
